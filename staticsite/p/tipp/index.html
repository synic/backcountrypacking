Training, Tuning & Tipp Horses

These horses are some of the best you will find. They are a part of the American Heritage association adoption program. I am a certified tipp trainer that works with these horses through basic gentling and breaking requirements so they can be adopted out. They are very smart and willing to go to work on whatever task you are asking of them. I usually have a few from which you can choose, or if you want to go and choose one with me at the BLM we can arrange to do so. The adoption fee for these horses is $125. Give me a call or email me and I would love to get you started with your new horse.

These are all currently sold. More will soon be coming.
