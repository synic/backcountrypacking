Have you ever wanted to pack with horses or mules? Learn the skills you’ll need from the very basics to begin packing, to leading two or more pack animals from Experienced professional Packers What you will learn. The function of a pack saddle and its uses: Learn the types of pack saddles, proper fitting-padding-Decker, Sawbucks & hybrids.

How to put on a pack saddle and lead a pack string:
Pack Mules and Horses will be supplied for you to practice on.

How to pack:
Setting pack boxes, manty’s, slings and bags. How to balance the load by putting the proper amount of weight inside. Learn how to manty a load without boxes or bags using a tarp and rope for odd size items. Help trouble shooting odd items.

Learn to tie different hitches:
Diamond, Double Diamond, Square Knot, Bowline,
Barrel, Basket hitch, Box hitch and the Knot that is not a Knot.

Learn how to prepare for your trip:
What you and your horse or mule will need in camp.

Trail Etiquette, Trail Dangers, and basic back country first aid for livestock:
Learn what to do when you meet someone on the trail. What common mistakes get people in wrecks.
How to prevent getting in a bad spot and what to do if you do.
What to do in an emergency.

Learn how to build your camp and camp etiquette:
Where do I camp? Where do I highline, put the latrine, graze my animals?
Forest service regulations for camping on State and Federal Land.

Basics of Dutch Oven Cooking:
Learn how to cook like a pro outside in cast iron Dutch Ovens.
Meal will be cooked with the Dutch Ovens by
Kent Walter
Master Chef
