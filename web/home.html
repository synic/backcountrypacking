Welcome to Back Country Packing!

In days past, any type of activity in the back country was done by packers and strings of horses, the only way to access these isolated locations. Today, things have changed a great deal, but the horse and reliable pack string still plays a significant role to pack campers, nature lovers, fishermen, and hunters into what is left of these secluded areas. A trail ride or pack trip is one of the most memorable experiences available. These first-class adventures are lead by highly skilled and friendly guides, who are happy to share their knowledge of the area as you travel the trails. We take great pride in our image, the quality of service we provide, and our commitment to customer satisfaction.
Authentic trail rides, horse packing trips, high alpine fishing trips, tent bed and breakfast trips, backpacking trips, and youth adventure camps.
