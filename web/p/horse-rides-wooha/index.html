Trail Rides

This is a unique opportunity, come and explore the mountains, canyons, and trails. These rides are not the normal structured trail ride like most places. We will assess your riding abilities, saddle up and go out and explore some of Utah’s best back country in the state. These back country rides offer some great riding opportunities from a top of some great mountain bred horses. This incredible country provides great trails, scenery, and chances of seeing wildlife. Look for deer, elk, and moose as you make your way through mountain trails. Our wranglers will lead you through some of Utah’s best horseback riding country and teach you riding skills as you make your way down the trail.

Full Day Rides $120 2 person minimum (lunch provided)

Half Day Rides $60 2 person minimum

Sunset Dinner Rides $80 2 person minimum (dinner provided)

These rates apply to Central Utah & Wasatch Mountain Range (for other areas call and we can get you a price)

What to bring: *Sunscreen/Hat *Long pants *Boots that go over ankle *Coat/Jacket *Water bottle *Camera
